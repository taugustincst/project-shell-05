## Full description
**A cozy deduction game about lost things.** You are the day-shift clerk at a railway lost-property office. Travellers come to your counter describing what they lost, from memory, badly: the wrong colour, the wrong train, the wrong day. One thing they never get wrong is the distinguishing mark.

**Three documents, one stamp.** Search the shelves, cross-check the found-item log, read the Standing Orders, and stamp: RETURN, HOLD, or FLAG. Any of them can mislead you, and the claimant is not the only one who makes mistakes. Every stamp is provable from the rules posted at your counter, and the rules grow through the season: insurance forms, closed entries, rival claimants, and the Thursday auction.

**A season with a story.** Thirty days of claimants, a budget for shelf labels and a kettle, and a lost wedding ring whose story arrives in pieces across a month, until you can put it back on the right hand.

- Free to play for the whole first week. One purchase unlocks the rest of the season; nothing else to buy, no ads, no accounts.
- Fully offline. Portrait, one-handed, large-text option.
- Thousands of generated objects; thirty authored story days.
