## Full description
**A cozy deduction puzzle game: run a railway lost-property office and send lost things home.**

**Listen, then check.** Travellers come to your counter describing what they lost, from memory, badly: the wrong colour, the wrong train, the wrong day. The one thing they never get wrong is the distinguishing mark. Search the shelves, read the tags, and find the one object that fits.

**Three sources, one stamp.** Cross-check the found-item log, read the Standing Orders, and stamp RETURN, HOLD or FLAG. The log has its own mistakes, and not every claimant is honest. Every stamp is provable from the rules posted at your counter.

**The rules grow through the season.** Eight Standing Orders are posted across thirty days: the calendar, closed entries, insurance forms, rival claimants and the Thursday auction. Prohibited articles join the list as the weeks go on.

**A season with a story.** A lost wedding ring's story arrives in pieces across the month, until you can put it back on the right hand. Twenty-five written characters bring their own small stories to the counter between the generated claims.

**Run the office.** Spend your budget between days on ten office upgrades, from shelf labels and index cards to a kettle, a counter lamp and a telephone. The season ends one of three ways, depending on how well you kept the counter.

- The first week (days 1 to 7) is free to play. One purchase unlocks the rest of the season; nothing else to buy.
- No ads, no accounts, no sign-in.
- Offline game: plays with no connection.
- Portrait, one-handed, with a large-text option.
- Every new season brings a new set of generated claimants.
