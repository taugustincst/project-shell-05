# itch.io page — Lost Property Office

A draft for a free web demo on itch.io. The web build plays the first week of
the season (days 1 to 7); the full season is the Google Play edition, so the
itch page works as a free demo that points to Play. Nothing here is published
yet: the page is created by hand, as below.

The zip is `lostproperty-itch.zip`, built with
`python3 itch_pack.py <repo> lostproperty-itch.zip` (the marketing tools). It
holds `index.html` at its root plus the files the page loads, and nothing from
the Android, test or store side. In a browser the unlock sheet says the full
season is unlocked in the Google Play edition.

## Upload

1. https://itch.io/game/new
2. **Kind of project:** HTML. Upload `lostproperty-itch.zip` and tick
   **This file will be played in the browser**.
3. **Embed options:** *Embed in page*; viewport **360 × 640** (portrait); tick
   **Mobile friendly** (orientation: *Portrait*) and **Fullscreen button**.
   SharedArrayBuffer: off (not used).
4. **Pricing:** *No payments* (free web demo). Never *Paid* here while the
   Google Play edition is the paid one.
5. **Visibility:** *Draft* until the page below is filled in and checked,
   then *Public*.

**Description** (itch takes Markdown)

    **Every lost thing has a story. Most of the stories are wrong.**

    You are the day-shift clerk at a railway lost-property office. Travellers
    come to your counter describing what they lost, from memory, badly: the
    wrong colour, the wrong train, the wrong day. The one thing they never get
    wrong is the distinguishing mark.

    **Search the shelves, read the tags, cross-check the found-item log, and
    stamp: RETURN, HOLD or FLAG.** The log has its own mistakes, and not every
    claimant is honest. Every stamp is provable from the Standing Orders posted
    at your counter.

    **This web version is the free first week of the season** (days 1 to 7):
    the first Standing Orders, the first prohibited articles, the first
    upgrades for the office, and the start of the story of a lost wedding ring.

    **The full season is on Google Play:** thirty days, eight Standing Orders
    (insurance forms, rival claimants, the Thursday auction), ten office
    upgrades, twenty-five written characters, the ring's story to the end, and
    three season endings.
    https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.taugustincst.lostproperty

    Portrait, one-handed, large-text option. No ads, no accounts.
