Lost Property Office

Lost Property Office is played in the Android app.

A cozy deduction game about lost things. You are the day-shift clerk at a railway lost-property office. Travellers come to your counter describing what they lost, from memory, badly: the wrong colour, the wrong train, the wrong day. One thing they never get wrong is the distinguishing mark.

Three documents, one stamp. Search the shelves, cross-check the found-item log, read the Standing Orders, and stamp: RETURN, HOLD, or FLAG. Any of the three can be lying, including the log. Every stamp is provable from the rules posted at your counter, and the rules grow through the season: insurance forms, closed entries, rival claimants, and the Thursday auction.

A season with a story. Thirty days of claimants, a budget for shelf labels and a kettle, and a lost wedding ring whose story arrives in pieces across a month, until you can put it back on the right hand.

  • Thousands of generated objects; thirty authored story days.
